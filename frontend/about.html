Our Core Mission

Cybercrime in India has evolved rapidly toward regional language social engineering. Vulnerable citizens frequently receive SMS threats in their mother tongue—such as Telugu power cutoff notices or lottery promises—that traditional English-only cybersecurity software fails to flag.

Digital Shield AI was engineered to bridge this critical language gap by deploying native Telugu (తెలుగు) and English NLP threat analysis, deep URL lexical security parsing, and QR code physical-to-digital fraud prevention into one accessible, connected platform.

Practical Playbooks

Cybersecurity Defense Guide

Actionable strategies to identify deceptive links, regional SMS traps, and predatory QR exploits.

1. Identifying Spoofed URLs

  • Subdomain Trickery: Attackers create domains like sbi.bank-login-secure.xyz. The actual registered domain is bank-login-secure.xyz, NOT SBI.
  • HTTPS Padlock Fallacy: A green lock icon only means the connection is encrypted; scammers get free SSL certificates too.
  • High-Risk TLDs: Be skeptical of unfamiliar extensions such as .xyz, .top, .biz, .work.

2. Regional Telugu Threat Patterns

  • Electricity Cutoff Scams: Messages claiming "రాత్రి 9:30కి విద్యుత్ సరఫరా నిలిపివేయబడుతుంది" are 100% fake. Power discoms never send disconnection threats with personal mobile numbers.
  • Fake Bank KYC Suspension: Banks like SBI, HDFC, or ICICI never send SMS demanding Aadhaar/PAN re-verification via third-party web forms.
  • Part-Time Telegram Job Scams: Promises of earning ₹3,000–₹8,000 daily for liking videos or rating hotels are advance-fee pyramid traps.

3. UPI & QR Code Defense Rules

  • The Cardinal Rule of UPI: You NEVER enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Entering your UPI PIN ALWAYS transfers money out of your account.
  • Quishing Stickers: Be vigilant of malicious QR stickers pasted over legitimate QR codes on merchant counters, parking meters, or petrol pumps.
  • Zero-Amount Collect Tricks: Fraudsters send flexible collect requests where you enter your PIN thinking you are confirming your KYC.
